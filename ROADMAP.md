## 3. After the jam / next version

**Next (follows the budget milestones):**
1. Polish: native Arabic review, original art and sound, live 2-phone racing.
2. More game: Abu Dhabi city, credit score, more lessons, classroom mode.
3. Launch: app stores, UAE school pilot, simple learning-progress view.

**Later:**

**Probably never:**
